Microarchitectural Masking: A JIT-Based Adaptive Dispatcher for Resource-Resilient Serverless Computing

Academic Research Paper · United International University
Ongoing
Designed and evaluated "Microarchitectural Masking", a zero-privilege, user-space JIT dispatcher that successfully neutralizes "Noisy Neighbor" interference in cloud hosts by dynamically shifting to hardware-pressure-optimized algorithmic kernels.
  • Ensured virtually no performance loss for compute/IO workloads under contention while recovering ~60% of memory-bound latency.
  • Mitigated an estimated 29.6kg of CO2e per million invocations seamlessly without requiring underlying infrastructure changes.

AI Driven Speech Based Depression Detection System

Academic Research Paper · United International University
Completed
Implemented machine learning and deep learning architectures to detect depression from vocal features, extracting robust vocal attributes utilizing openSMILE feature extraction tools and methodologies.
  • Built predictive models using SVM, XGBoost, CNN, RNN, LSTM, and GMM models.
  • Evaluated and analyzed system performance using Weighted Average Recall (WAR) and conducted comparative model analysis to optimize predictive reliability.
